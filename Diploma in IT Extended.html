Diploma In Information Technology:Extended

Preparing future professionals with the skills employers demand in software, networks, and systems.

APPLY THROUGH CAO

Program Overview

The Diploma in IT:Extended program equips students with advanced computing skills including programming, databases, networking, and systems analysis. Graduates are prepared for diverse careers in the digital world.

Entry Requirements

  • National Senior Certificate or equivalent
  • English First Additional Language(3)OR
  • English Home Language(3)AND
  • Mathematics(2)OR
  • Mathematics Literacy(3)
  • Atleast 50% in three fundamental subjects including English
  • Atleast 60% in three complusory vocational subjects

Duration Of Study

  • 4 years full-time

Career Paths

  • Software Developer
  • Systems Analyst
  • Network Engineer
  • IT Consultant
  • Database Administrator

Key Skills You Will Gain

Programming

Master languages like Java, Python, and C# to build real-world software.

Networking

Learn how to design, configure, and secure modern networks.

Database Management

Understand database systems, SQL, and data analysis techniques.
