Diploma in Information Technology

A practical, hands‑on program to prepare students for real‑world IT careers with essential tech skills.

APPLY THROUGH CAO

Program Overview

The Diploma in Information Technology focuses on practical skills needed in industry such as technical support, basics of programming, networking, and database management. This course equips students with job‑ready abilities to enter the IT workforce with confidence.

Admission Requirements

  • English First Additional Language(3)OR
  • English Home Language(3)AND
  • Mathematics(3)OR
  • Mathematics Literacy(5)
  • Atleast 50% in three fundamental subjects including English
  • Atleast 60% in three complusory vocational subjects

Duration Of Study

  • 3 years full-time

Skills You Will Gain

  • Computer troubleshooting and maintenance
  • Web and software basics
  • Database foundations
  • Networking principles
  • Technical communication skills

Career Opportunities

  • IT Support Specialist
  • Junior Web Developer
  • Network Technician
  • Database Assistant
  • Help Desk Technician

Industry‑Focused Training

This diploma prepares students to enter real‑world IT environments with hands‑on experience and industry‑relevant skills.
